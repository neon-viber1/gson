Reject null elements in AtomicIntegerArray with a JsonSyntaxException

Reading "[1,null,3]" as AtomicIntegerArray leaked an IllegalStateException
from JsonReader.nextInt(). Throw a JsonSyntaxException with the path, as the
AtomicLongArray adapter already does.
